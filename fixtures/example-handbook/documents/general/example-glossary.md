[600.01] # Terms

[600.01(1)] A "handoff" is the transfer of control of an aircraft from one position to the next.

[600.01(2)] A "release" is permission from one position for another to act in its airspace.

[600.01(2)(a)] A blanket release applies to a defined set of aircraft until withdrawn.
